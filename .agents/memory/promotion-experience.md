---
name: Landing promotion experience
description: Product decisions for the public landing page's promotional modal and feature rail
---

The public landing experience uses one featured promotion modal only; the detailed promotional content lives in a swipeable feature rail beneath the four hero data windows.

**Why:** A single focused modal is easier to dismiss and understand, while the rail can communicate EduTrack's broader feature set without stacking multiple popups or interrupting the hero.

**How to apply:** Keep the modal prominent but brief, use a large touch-friendly close control, use Bengali-first copy and Hind Siliguri, and keep the rail smooth on both desktop and mobile with touch swiping and reduced-motion support. Use a dedicated wide rail asset/card ratio on desktop and retain a square source fallback on mobile so photography is not vertically cropped.

---
name: Landing glass surfaces
description: Visual boundary for EduTrack's public landing-page glass treatment
---

Use restrained transparent glass only on the public landing narrative: one continuous blue/lilac ambient canvas, layered translucency, blur, inset highlights, selective depth, and deep indigo glass for formerly dark promotional/comparison/footer surfaces. Keep the authenticated product shell on its established operational visual language.

**Why:** The landing page can communicate premium trust and modernity through atmosphere, while the enterprise app must preserve clarity, density, familiar controls, and task focus.

**How to apply:** Scope future glass rules to landing-page selectors, let ambient color pass through section boundaries, preserve existing content/CTA behavior and reduced-motion handling, and verify desktop/mobile contrast before publishing.

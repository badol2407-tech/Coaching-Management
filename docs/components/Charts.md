# Charts

## Purpose

Use a Chart when a visual encoding of quantitative data supports a decision that a table or prose summary alone would not support as well. Charts in EduTrack communicate trends, distributions, and comparisons for Attendance, Fees, Exams, Analytics, and Reports — they do not decorate dashboards or imply precision the data does not support.

## Non-goals

Do not use a Chart when exact values are the primary need — use a [Table](./Table.md) or metric display. Do not use 3D perspective, dual axes without a compelling reason, or truncated axes that exaggerate differences. Do not use a chart as the only representation of critical status — charts are supplemental; the authoritative data lives in the record set. Do not use a Chart for AI-generated Analytics without labeling the source scope and generation state.

## Anatomy and variants

Every chart must provide a title, data scope label (organization, batch, subject, date range), axis labels with units, a legend when multiple series are present, and a data table or text summary as an accessible equivalent.

Support these variants aligned with the visualization contract in [DATA_VISUALIZATION_GUIDE.md](../DATA_VISUALIZATION_GUIDE.md):

- **Bar chart** — comparable categories; used for Fee collection by month, Attendance by batch, or Exam subject comparison.
- **Line chart** — time trends; used for Attendance rate over term, Fee collection trajectory, or Exam result trend.
- **Donut or pie chart** — part-to-whole; used sparingly and only when the whole is meaningful (e.g., Fee status breakdown: paid, outstanding, waived). Limit to five or fewer segments.
- **Metric tile** — a single value, unit, period, comparison baseline, and status indicator; used in Dashboard widgets and Report summaries.
- **Stacked bar** — cumulative composition over categories; used for Attendance status breakdown (present, absent, late) per session.
- **Scatter or distribution** — use only when the audience can interpret the encoding and a simpler chart does not serve the decision.

## States and behavior

Support loading, populated, empty, stale, error, and AI-generated states.

- **Loading:** Show a dimensionally stable skeleton in place of the chart area; do not show axes without data.
- **Empty or no data:** Distinguish "no records in this scope" from "data is still loading" from "filters exclude all data." Follow [EMPTY_STATES.md](../EMPTY_STATES.md).
- **Stale:** Show a freshness indicator with the last-updated timestamp when data may not reflect the current state.
- **AI-generated:** Label the chart with "Generated by AI — review before acting" and show source scope. Do not present AI estimates as recorded facts.
- **Interaction:** Tooltips supplement but do not replace visible labels. Critical values must be readable without hover. Drill-down from a chart navigates to the underlying record set with the same scope applied.
- **Export:** Exported charts include title, scope, date range, units, and generation timestamp.

## Accessibility and responsive behavior

Provide an accessible text summary of the chart's key finding — not just a data dump — as `aria-label` on the chart container or as an adjacent description. Provide a full data table accessible to screen readers. Use color combined with patterns, labels, or direct annotations; never use color alone for series distinction. Maintain WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for all text and chart elements against their background.

Announce result updates with an appropriate live region when Filters or date ranges change. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — transitions are informational, not required for comprehension.

At narrow viewports, simplify the chart to the most critical series; provide a link to the full table. Follow [RESPONSIVE_SYSTEM.md](../RESPONSIVE_SYSTEM.md) and [DATA_VISUALIZATION_GUIDE.md](../DATA_VISUALIZATION_GUIDE.md).

## Content and examples

Chart title: "Attendance rate — Batch A, all subjects, April–June 2026." Metric tile: "Fee collection rate — 78% of ₹4,20,000 billed — as of 1 August 2026." Tooltip: "May 2026: 312 present of 380 enrolled (82%)." AI label: "AI-generated trend estimate — based on records through 28 July 2026."

## Review evidence

Verify chart title, scope, units, and freshness are present; data table or text summary is available to screen readers; color is not the only series distinction; missing, stale, and zero data are distinguishable from populated data; AI-generated charts are labeled; tooltips do not carry the only instance of critical values; drill-down preserves scope context; export includes all metadata; reduced motion is respected; narrow viewport falls back gracefully; zoom to 200% does not lose axis labels or legend.
